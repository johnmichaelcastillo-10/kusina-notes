Why a new look

Enamelware and the palengke

The first draft looked like Terra & Thread. This one borrows from a Filipino home kitchen instead: white enamel plates with blue rims, hand-lettered market signs, gingham oilcloth on the table.

Portfolio

Calm monochrome

No hue at all. Grotesk type, soft cards, product polish.

Terra & Thread

Quiet and earthy

Parchment, clay and sage. Serif headings, fine rules, lots of air.

Kusina Notes

Loud, warm, funny

Enamel white, cobalt rims, a bright colour for every course, and a tita's voice.

Colour

Every course gets its own colour

The base is enamel and navy ink. A recipe page takes its course's colour for the header band and step numbers, so the site gets louder without one-off decoration.

    Voice

    Guard rails

    Loud chrome, quiet recipes

    Do

    • One or two playful details per view: a tilted tag, gingham or handwriting, not all three.
    • Keep tag rotations fixed per tag type so pages and screenshots stay stable.
    • Gingham only in the hero and footer.
    • Overhead CC0 photos, so they crop well as plates.
    • Print in plain black on white.

    Don't

    • Decorate inside the ingredient list or method text.
    • Use course colours as text on enamel (several fail contrast there).
    • Gradients, glass effects, emoji icons, cartoon chefs.
    • Invented ratings, reviews or "readers love this".
    • Wiggling or animated tags.
